Start with the district, then the address

Public schools in ZIP 66223 are served by Blue Valley USD 229. That's the starting point, but a ZIP code doesn't tell you which elementary, middle or high school a particular home is assigned to. Attendance boundaries are drawn by the district and follow streets and neighborhoods, not ZIP lines.

How to confirm your attendance area

  1. Get the full street address of every home you're seriously considering.
  2. Use the district's official boundary or school-locator tools on the Blue Valley USD 229 website, or contact the district directly.
  3. Ask whether any boundary changes are under review or planned. Growing districts sometimes adjust boundaries as new schools open or enrollment shifts.
  4. Save the confirmation in writing or as a dated screenshot, along with the date you checked.
Don't rely on listings: real estate listings sometimes include school information, but it can be outdated or wrong. Only the district can confirm current attendance assignments for an address. This site isn't affiliated with Blue Valley USD 229 or any school.

Questions to ask on a school visit

Call the school office to ask about visits or tours. Policies vary, so ask how they handle prospective families. Bring a list:

For the principal or front office

  • What makes this school different from others in the district?
  • What are typical class sizes in my child's grade?
  • How do you support students who are ahead and students who are struggling?
  • How do you handle bullying, discipline and student safety?
  • What does a typical day look like, including recess, lunch and specials?
  • How are new students welcomed mid-year?

For teachers or counselors

  • How much homework is typical at this grade?
  • How do you communicate with parents, and how often?
  • What technology do students use, and is it provided?
  • For middle and high school: how does course selection work, and what advanced options exist?

For current parents

  • What do you like most, and what would you change?
  • How involved is the parent community, and how easy is it to get involved?
  • How does the school handle concerns when you raise them?

Watch the details during a visit: how students and staff interact in hallways, how classrooms look and whether students seem engaged.

Enrollment: documents you'll typically need

Requirements are set by the district and can change, so confirm the current list with Blue Valley USD 229 before you enroll. Families are typically asked for items like these:

  • Proof of residency in the attendance area, such as a closing statement, signed lease or recent utility bill. Some districts have specific rules for families who are still in the middle of a move.
  • Child's birth certificate or other proof of age.
  • Immunization records. Ask what Kansas and the district require for your child's grade.
  • Parent or guardian identification.
  • Previous school records, such as report cards, transcripts and withdrawal forms from the prior school.
  • Special services documents, such as a current IEP or 504 plan, if applicable.
  • Custody or guardianship paperwork, if applicable.
Timing tip: start a folder (paper and digital) as soon as you decide to move. Request records from your current school before you leave, and ask the new district about enrollment windows, especially for kindergarten and for mid-year transfers.

Frequently asked questions

Which school district serves 66223?

Blue Valley USD 229 serves the 66223 ZIP code. Your specific assigned schools depend on your exact street address, so confirm with the district.

Can I trust the school information on a home listing?

Treat it as a starting point only. Listings can be outdated or wrong, and boundaries can change. Confirm attendance assignments for the specific address directly with Blue Valley USD 229.

What do I need to enroll my child?

Typically proof of residency, proof of your child's age, immunization records, parent identification and prior school records, plus IEP, 504 or custody documents if they apply. Check the district's current requirements before you go.

Can I enroll before I've closed on a house?

Policies on enrolling during a move vary. Ask the district what documentation it accepts, such as a signed purchase contract or lease, and what timing rules apply.

Should I choose a school based on rankings?

Rankings can be one input, but they rarely capture class sizes, services, programs or fit for your child. Combine official data, school visits and conversations with current parents.
