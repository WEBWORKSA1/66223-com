1. Utilities

Most homes in 66223 involve four separate providers. Call or go online to start or transfer service, and write down account numbers as you go.

  • [ ] Electric: Evergy
  • [ ] Water: WaterOne
  • [ ] Natural gas: Kansas Gas Service
  • [ ] Sewer: Johnson County Wastewater
  • [ ] Ask whether a deposit is required, and how to have it waived with a good payment history if that is an option.
  • [ ] Schedule service to start on or before your move-in date, and stop service at your old address the day after you leave.
  • [ ] Take meter photos on move-in day for your records.

Have ready: your new address, move-in date, ID and a lease or closing documents.

4. Driver's license and vehicles

Kansas has its own requirements for new residents. Rather than rely on secondhand advice, confirm current rules and deadlines with the official sources.

  • [ ] Check the Kansas Department of Revenue for current driver's license requirements and timelines for new residents.
  • [ ] Check Johnson County for vehicle title and registration steps, required documents and any inspection requirements for out-of-state vehicles.
  • [ ] Gather documents commonly requested: current license, proof of identity, proof of Kansas residency, vehicle title or lienholder information and proof of insurance. Use the official list as the final word.
  • [ ] Update your auto insurance to your new address and Kansas coverage.
  • [ ] Book appointments early if they are offered. Walk-in waits can be long.
No guesswork on deadlines: Deadlines for new residents to get a license and register vehicles are set by the state and can change. Confirm them with the Kansas Department of Revenue and Johnson County as soon as you move.

8. Safety and weather prep

Spring and early summer bring thunderstorms, hail and tornadoes to Kansas. Prepare in your first weeks, not the night of a warning.

  • [ ] Pick your shelter spot: the lowest level of the home, in an interior room away from windows.
  • [ ] Turn on emergency alerts on every phone in the household and follow forecasts from the National Weather Service.
  • [ ] Build a kit: flashlights, batteries, water, a first-aid kit, phone chargers, medications and sturdy shoes.
  • [ ] Review your homeowner's or renter's insurance for wind and hail coverage and deductibles. See our hail, roof and insurance guide.
  • [ ] Test smoke and carbon monoxide detectors, and locate your main water shutoff and electrical panel.
  • [ ] Change or rekey exterior locks if you bought the home.
  • [ ] Save non-emergency numbers for police and fire, and find your nearest urgent care and hospital.

Frequently asked questions

Who provides utilities in 66223?

Common providers are Evergy for electric, WaterOne for water, Kansas Gas Service for natural gas and Johnson County Wastewater for sewer. Trash is typically handled by private haulers or through an HOA.

How long do I have to get a Kansas driver's license after moving?

Check the Kansas Department of Revenue for the current requirement. Rules for new residents can change, so rely on the official source rather than general advice.

How do I enroll my child in school in 66223?

Verify your assigned school with Blue Valley USD 229 using your exact address, then follow the district's current enrollment steps and document list.

Do I need to arrange trash pickup myself?

Possibly. Trash in the area is typically handled by private haulers or arranged by HOAs. Ask your HOA, landlord or seller what applies to your home.

When should I prepare for severe weather?

As soon as you move in. Severe storms, hail and tornadoes are most common in spring and early summer, so have a shelter plan and alerts set up before then.
