Licensing and registration: verify before you open

Requirements depend on your business type, structure and location, and they change. Use this as a checklist of questions, then confirm every item with the official source.

  1. Choose a structure: sole proprietorship, LLC or corporation. Talk to an accountant or attorney about liability and taxes.
  2. Register with the State of Kansas if your structure requires it, and check name availability.
  3. Get an EIN from the IRS if you will have employees or form an entity that needs one.
  4. Sales tax: if you sell taxable goods or services, register with the Kansas Department of Revenue. The combined sales tax rate in Overland Park is 9.35%; confirm what applies to your sales.
  5. City requirements: check with the City of Overland Park about business licensing, zoning, signs, home-based business rules and any trade or contractor licensing.
  6. County requirements: some trades and permits involve Johnson County. Ask which office covers your work.
  7. Insurance: general liability at minimum; add workers' compensation, commercial auto or professional liability as your work requires.
Home-based business? Check your HOA covenants as well as city rules. Many 66223 subdivisions restrict signage, customer traffic, parking and commercial vehicles.

Local marketing that works

Google Business Profile

For most local businesses, your Google Business Profile drives more calls than your website. Set it up and keep it current:

  • Choose the most accurate primary category, then add relevant secondary categories.
  • Set your service area to the ZIPs and cities you actually serve.
  • Keep hours accurate, including holiday hours.
  • Upload real photos of your work, team and vehicles, and add new ones regularly.
  • List services with plain descriptions and price ranges where you can.
  • Post updates for seasonal offers, such as spring storm inspections or fall gutter cleaning.

Reviews

Reviews are the trust signal local customers check first. Build a simple system:

  • Ask every satisfied customer, right after the job, with a direct link.
  • Reply to every review, positive or negative, politely and specifically.
  • Handle a bad review by acknowledging it, offering to fix the problem offline, and following through.
  • Never buy reviews or offer rewards for positive ones. It breaks platform rules and erodes trust.

Community sponsorships and presence

Overland Park has more than 80 city parks and well-attended community spots such as the Overland Park Farmers' Market and Deanna Rose Children's Farmstead. Community involvement builds name recognition that ads cannot buy:

  • Sponsor youth sports teams, school fundraisers or neighborhood events. Ask organizers about official sponsorship options and rules.
  • Offer HOA newsletter tips or a seasonal checklist, with permission from the HOA.
  • Partner with complementary businesses for referrals, such as a realtor and a home inspector, or a landscaper and an irrigation pro.

Your website

Keep it fast, mobile-friendly and specific: what you do, where you serve, typical price ranges, how to book, and proof of licensing and insurance. One clear page per core service beats a vague all-in-one page.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a business license in Overland Park?

It depends on your business type and location. Check directly with the City of Overland Park and the State of Kansas before you start operating.

Do I have to collect sales tax?

If you sell taxable goods or services, generally yes. The combined rate in Overland Park is 9.35%. Confirm your obligations with the Kansas Department of Revenue.

Can I run a business from my home in 66223?

Often, but city zoning rules and HOA covenants can limit signage, traffic, parking and vehicles. Check both before you begin.

What is the fastest way to get local customers?

A complete Google Business Profile, a steady stream of genuine reviews and fast responses to inquiries usually deliver the quickest results.
