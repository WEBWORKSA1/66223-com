Why 66223?

ZIP 66223 covers part of southern Overland Park in Johnson County, Kansas — a suburban corner of the Kansas City metro that keeps showing up on national lists. Niche named Overland Park the #10 best city to live in America for 2026, and WalletHub ranked it the #2 place in the country to raise a family. Inside the ZIP, roughly 25,000 people live in about 9,800 households, the median household income is around $113,700, and about two-thirds of adults hold a bachelor's degree or higher.

Those numbers come with real decisions attached: how much house to buy when values have risen from about $263,000 in 2011 to about $437,500, how Kansas property taxes actually work, whether to rent first, which school boundary a specific address falls in, and how to protect a roof in hail country. 66223.com exists to answer those questions clearly — and to connect you with people who can help when you're ready.
