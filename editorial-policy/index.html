Policy

Editorial policy

Last updated October 21, 2026.

Accuracy

Every statistic we publish links to, or names, a public source. When sources disagree, we say so. We review guides at least twice a year and when rules change. Numbers we can't verify are presented as clearly labeled rules of thumb or examples.

Independence

Advertisers and sponsors cannot buy coverage, rankings or edits. Sponsored content is labeled “Sponsored” or “Partner”. Referral relationships never determine what a guide recommends.

Not professional advice

We explain how things work so you can ask better questions. For legal, tax, lending, insurance or real-estate decisions, consult a licensed professional.

Corrections

Spot an error? Tell us through the contact page. We correct material errors promptly and note significant updates on the page.

Third-party content

We embed YouTube videos created by others with credit and our own commentary. Creators can request removal at any time.
