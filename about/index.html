About 66223.com

Last updated October 21, 2026.

66223.com is an independent, people-first guide to ZIP 66223 — the southern part of Overland Park, Kansas. We help newcomers decide whether to move here, help residents make better money decisions about their homes, and connect people with trustworthy local pros.

What we publish

  • Guides — practical, sourced explainers on moving, property taxes, buying and selling, home maintenance, schools and family life.
  • Calculators — mortgage, Kansas property tax, affordability, rent vs. buy, moving cost and cost of living, preloaded with local data.
  • Data — a sourced snapshot of 66223 we keep updated.
  • Community programs — contests, pro nominations and local talent opportunities.

How we make money

We show advertising served by Google AdSense, offer clearly labeled sponsorships, and may receive referral fees when we connect a reader with a local professional at the reader's request. Reader contributions support operations. None of this changes what we write; see our editorial policy.

Independence

We are not affiliated with the City of Overland Park, Johnson County, the U.S. Postal Service, Blue Valley USD 229, or any business mentioned on this site.
