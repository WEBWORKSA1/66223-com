The Kansas formula

Annual tax = appraised value × 11.5% × total mills ÷ 1,000 − school-levy exemption. The exemption removes the first $75,000 of a home's value from the statewide 20-mill school levy, worth about $172.50 per year. Overland Park's own city levy (about 14.5 mills for 2026) is the lowest of any Johnson County city and makes up only around 13% of a typical bill — the rest goes to the county, school district, state and other districts.
