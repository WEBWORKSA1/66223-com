How this calculator works

Principal and interest use the standard amortization formula. Property tax uses Kansas rules: residential property is assessed at 11.5% of appraised value, multiplied by your total mill levy and divided by 1,000, minus the exemption on the first $75,000 of value for the statewide 20-mill school levy. The default 120 mills is illustrative — your actual levy depends on the exact taxing districts on your tax statement.

PMI is shown at an illustrative 0.6% of the loan per year when the down payment is below 20%; real PMI depends on credit score and lender.
