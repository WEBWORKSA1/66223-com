What the model assumes

The buyer pays about 3% in closing costs, owns the home for the years you choose, then sells with roughly 7% in selling costs. The renter invests the would-be down payment and closing costs, plus any monthly savings versus owning, at the return you set. Change any input to stress-test the answer — appreciation and how long you stay usually matter most.
