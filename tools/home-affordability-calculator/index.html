Rules of thumb, not rules

The comfortable figure keeps housing costs at or below 28% of gross monthly income and total debt payments at or below 36%. The stretch figure uses 33% / 43%, closer to what some lenders will approve. Approval is not the same as comfort — leave room for maintenance, childcare and savings.
