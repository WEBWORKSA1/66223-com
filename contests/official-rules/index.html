Official rules

“66223 in Focus” Season 1 — official rules

Please read these rules before entering. By entering you agree to them.

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A PURCHASE OR DONATION WILL NOT INCREASE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED.

1. Sponsor

The contest is sponsored by the publisher of 66223.com (“Sponsor”). Contact the Sponsor through the contact page. The contest is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by any city, school district, social network or photo-hosting service.

2. Eligibility

Open to legal residents of the 50 United States and D.C. who are 18 or older at the time of entry. Employees and contractors of Sponsor and their immediate household members are not eligible.

3. Entry period

Entries are accepted from November 1, 2026 through January 31, 2027, 11:59 p.m. Central Time. Sponsor's clock is the official timekeeper.

4. How to enter

Submit the entry form on the contest page with a viewable link to an original photograph. Limit three (3) entries per person. Photos must be taken in or near ZIP 66223 / Overland Park, Kansas.

5. Entry requirements

  • You must be the sole author and copyright owner of the photo.
  • You must have permission from any identifiable person pictured (and from a parent or guardian for any minor).
  • No nudity, hateful content, trademarks used as the main subject, or content that violates any law or third-party right.
  • Light editing is fine; composites must be disclosed.

6. Judging

A panel selected by Sponsor will score eligible entries: originality (40%), sense of place (30%), technical quality (30%). Ties are broken by the originality score. Judges' decisions are final.

7. Prizes

First place: one $250 gift card. Second place: one $100 gift card. Third place: one $50 gift card. Gift card brand chosen by Sponsor. Approximate retail value of all prizes: $400. Prizes may be funded by named contest sponsors. No cash substitution or transfer except at Sponsor's discretion; Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for any taxes.

8. Winner notification

Winners will be notified by email within 30 days after the entry period ends and must respond within 14 days, or an alternate winner may be selected.

9. License to use your photo

You keep your copyright. By entering, you grant Sponsor a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to display your photo, with credit, on 66223.com and its social channels in connection with the contest and the site, for up to three years.

10. Privacy

Entry information is used to run the contest and contact winners, as described in our Privacy Policy.

11. General conditions

Sponsor may cancel, modify or suspend the contest if fraud, technical failure or other factors impair its integrity, and may disqualify entries that break these rules. By entering, entrants release Sponsor from liability for any injury or loss arising from participation or prize use, to the extent permitted by law.

12. Winners list

Winners will be posted on the contest page after verification.
