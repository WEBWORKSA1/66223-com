Why Overland Park works for families

Overland Park consistently shows up on national lists for family life. WalletHub ranked it the #2 best place to raise a family in 2026, behind only Fremont, California, and Niche placed it #10 on its 2026 list of the Best Cities to Live in America. Rankings don't plan your Saturday, though. What matters day to day is that the city's best family spots are close, well maintained and good for a wide range of ages.

One practical note: 66223 has a Walk Score of 33, which means it's car-dependent. Most outings mean loading up the car, so the guide below includes tips on timing and what to bring.

The big three

Overland Park Arboretum & Botanical Gardens

The Arboretum is the place to go when you want kids outside and moving without a playground. It's a large natural area with gardens and trails, and the LongHouse Visitor Center is a handy starting point for orientation, restrooms and a break.

  • Best for: nature walks, toddlers who love to explore, family photos, grandparents visiting from out of town.
  • Make it a game: bring a simple scavenger list (a red flower, a bird, a bridge, a smooth rock, something taller than Dad).
  • Bring: water, sunscreen, bug spray in summer and sturdy shoes. A wagon or jogging stroller handles trails better than a lightweight umbrella stroller.
  • Before you go: check the city's site for current hours, admission and any seasonal events or closures.

Deanna Rose Children's Farmstead

The Farmstead is a favorite with younger kids, and for many local families it's a rite of passage. Expect farm animals and a hands-on, kid-scaled setting built around rural life.

  • Best for: toddlers through early elementary ages, first outings with new friends, birthday-week treats.
  • Timing tip: go early in the day, especially in summer. It's cooler, animals tend to be more active and it's typically less crowded.
  • Bring: hand sanitizer or wipes (you'll be touching animals and fences), a change of clothes for little ones and snacks.
  • Before you go: it typically runs on a seasonal schedule, so confirm opening dates, hours and fees on the official city site.

Overland Park Farmers' Market

The Farmers' Market took first prize in the 2022 America's Farmers Market Celebration, and it's an easy way to build a weekend morning routine. Kids get to see where food comes from, and you get fresh produce for the week.

  • Best for: all ages, slow weekend mornings, teaching kids about money and food.
  • Kid budget idea: give each child a few dollars to spend on one item they choose themselves, then cook it together that week.
  • Bring: reusable bags, small bills and a cooler in the car for hot days.
  • Before you go: check market days, hours and the season's start and end dates with the city.
Always verify: hours, admission, parking and event schedules change by season and year. Check the City of Overland Park website before you head out. This guide isn't affiliated with the city.

Making the most of 80+ city parks

Overland Park has more than 80 city parks, which is the best low-cost entertainment you'll find. Turn it into a project:

  • The park passport: make a simple checklist or map and let kids put a sticker on each new park you visit. Rate playgrounds on a 1 to 5 scale.
  • Pick parks by need: shade and restrooms for toddlers, open fields for kite flying and ball games, trails for bikes and scooters.
  • Plan a loop: combine a park with a library stop or a grocery run so the drive does double duty.
  • Use the city's park finder on the official site to check amenities like shelters, splash features and accessible play equipment.

Weather moves fast in Kansas. Spring and early summer bring thunderstorms and the occasional severe weather warning, so check the National Weather Service forecast before longer outdoor outings and have an indoor backup plan.

Frequently asked questions

Is Deanna Rose Children's Farmstead open year-round?

It typically operates on a seasonal schedule. Opening dates, hours and fees can change, so check the City of Overland Park website before you go.

What ages is the Arboretum good for?

All ages. Toddlers enjoy exploring paths and gardens, older kids can handle longer trails, and it's a relaxed outing for grandparents. Bring a sturdy stroller or wagon for little ones.

How many parks does Overland Park have?

The city has more than 80 parks. Use the official city website to find their locations and amenities such as shelters, restrooms and play equipment.

When is the Overland Park Farmers' Market open?

Market days and seasons are set by the city and can change from year to year. Check the City of Overland Park website for the current schedule.

Do I need a car to get around for family outings?

In most cases, yes. 66223 has a Walk Score of 33, which rates it car-dependent, so plan for driving and parking for most outings.
