Privacy & cookie policy

Last updated October 21, 2026.

This policy explains what information 66223.com (“we”, “us”) collects, how we use it, and your choices.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (matching requests, contest entries, applications, newsletter, sponsorship or support inquiries) we collect what you enter — such as name, email, phone, ZIP and details of your request. Form submissions are delivered to us through a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit) and stored in our email system.

How we use it

  • To respond to you and provide what you asked for.
  • For matching requests: to share your request with up to three relevant local professionals, only when you give consent on the form.
  • To run contests and contact winners.
  • To send the newsletter if you subscribe (unsubscribe anytime).

We do not sell your personal information to data brokers or list sellers.

Advertising and cookies (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland will be shown a consent message managed through Google's certified consent tools before personalized ads are served.

Other cookies and storage

We store small preferences in your browser (theme, cookie choice). Embedded YouTube videos load only when you click play and use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode; YouTube's own policies then apply. Fonts are served by Google Fonts.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete personal information you submitted, or to stop contacting you, through the contact page. Residents of states with privacy laws (such as California) may have additional rights, including to know and delete information and to opt out of “sale” or “sharing”; we honor these requests.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and contests are limited to people 18 and older.

Retention and security

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose collected or as required by law, and use reasonable safeguards. No method of transmission is perfectly secure.

Changes

We will post updates here with a new “last updated” date.
